About these models

The galaxy models use the constant-Lagrangian metric-inflow picture: the bulge sets an inflow of space whose orbital and radial channels add up to a constant vL², and spiral arms are streamlines of that flow. The jet models use the three-rapidity Painlevé–Gullstrand metric, in which flow lines lie on cones of fixed polar angle. The riverbed model shows how space cells are created and absorbed, and how the active riverbed moves matter.

Each model separates what is imposed by observation, calibrated, derived from the geometry, or interpretive, and states its display conventions (for example, jet cones drawn wider than their true angle so the helices are visible).

The SPARC explorers use the SPARC database (Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016) and the single-Lagrangian CL fits. Their numbers can be reproduced with the code and data in SPARC-CL-scaling, archived at doi:10.5281/zenodo.23340027.
