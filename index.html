E.P.J. de Haas · Kandinsky College, Nijmegen

Galaxies and jets as flows of space

The constant-Lagrangian metric-inflow galaxy model and the three-rapidity Painlevé–Gullstrand jet geometry: interactive models to explore, videos that show the flow, and fits of the model to all 175 SPARC rotation curves.

  • Interactive models

    Each model runs the equations live in your browser. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and change the parameters yourself.

    • Spiral galaxy with polar jets
    • The Hubble sequence from cone occupation
    • The M 87 jet in a single PG geometry
    • The riverbed of space
    Open the models

  • Videos

    Animations of how space flows through a galaxy, step-by-step fits to SPARC rotation curves, and polar jets in 3D. The Python scripts that render them are open.

    • The galaxy model: inflow, nested spirals, the Hubble sequence
    • Fitting real rotation curves: one Lagrangian, two, and an offset
    • Jets: Painlevé–Gullstrand cones and the jet of M 87
    Watch the videos
